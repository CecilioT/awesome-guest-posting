# Awesome Guest Posting [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of marketplaces, tools, guides, and resources for guest posting and editorial link building.

Guest posting (contributing articles to other websites in exchange for author bylines, exposure, or backlinks) remains one of the most reliable white-hat link building tactics. The landscape is fragmented across marketplaces, outreach software, journalist platforms, and niche communities. This list tries to map it honestly, with working links.

Entries are listed alphabetically within each section. No entry is "number one" by position. Inclusion here is not an endorsement of every feature; read current reviews before you buy.

## Marketplaces

Platforms where buyers and publishers transact guest post placements, link insertions, or sponsored articles.

- [Adbassador](https://adbassador.com) - Vetted marketplace for guest posts, link insertions, banner ads, and press releases with detailed publisher metrics.
- [Adsy](https://adsy.com) - Marketplace connecting publishers and buyers for sponsored posts.
- [BackLinked](https://backlinked.com) - Managed link building service with a marketplace interface.
- [BlogDrip](https://blogdrip.com) - Global guest post and link insertion marketplace with publishers in 60+ countries and self-service campaigns.
- [Collaborator.pro](https://collaborator.pro) - International sponsored content platform with publisher traffic and authority data.
- [Getfluence](https://getfluence.com) - Marketplace for sponsored articles on European and international publications.
- [Linkhouse](https://linkhouse.net) - Content marketplace with 74,000+ publishers, strong European inventory.
- [Publisuites](https://publisuites.com) - Spanish-language-heavy marketplace for sponsored posts, reviews, and social mentions.
- [PRNEWS.IO](https://prnews.io) - Sponsored content and press release network with a news-site focus.
- [WhitePress](https://whitepress.com) - Large European content marketplace with an extensive publisher database.
  

## Outreach Tools

Software for pitching editors, tracking replies, and managing guest post campaigns at scale.

- [BuzzStream](https://buzzstream.com) - Link building CRM with outreach sequencing and relationship tracking.
- [Hunter Campaigns](https://hunter.io/campaigns) - Email outreach built into the Hunter email-finding workflow.
- [Lemlist](https://lemlist.com) - Cold email and outreach platform with personalization at scale.
- [Mailshake](https://mailshake.com) - Cold outreach and follow-up automation used for link building and sales.
- [Pitchbox](https://pitchbox.com) - Outreach and link building platform used by agencies and in-house SEO teams.
- [Postaga](https://postaga.com) - All-in-one outreach platform with built-in prospecting.
- [Respona](https://respona.com) - Outreach and digital PR platform with journalist discovery features.

## Prospecting and Backlink Analysis

Tools for finding guest post opportunities, analyzing competitor backlinks, and qualifying target sites.

- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) - Backlink index, site audit, and content gap analysis; the Content Explorer tool is useful for finding sites that publish on your topic.
- [BuzzSumo](https://buzzsumo.com) - Content and journalist discovery, useful for finding writers who cover your niche.
- [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io) - Email finder for reaching editors directly.
- [Majestic](https://majestic.com) - Backlink analysis with Trust Flow and Topical Trust Flow metrics.
- [Moz Pro](https://moz.com/products/pro) - SEO toolset including Link Explorer for backlink discovery.
- [NinjaOutreach](https://ninjaoutreach.com) - Influencer and blogger discovery platform for outreach prospecting.
- [Semrush Backlink Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/backlinks/) - Backlink and link-gap analysis across competitors.
- [Ubersuggest](https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/) - Lower-cost SEO toolset with backlink data and content ideas.

## Journalist and HARO-Style Platforms

Platforms that connect sources to journalists, often producing contributor bylines or quote placements rather than traditional guest posts. Useful adjacent tactic.

- [Featured](https://featured.com) - Expert-sourcing platform (formerly Terkel) connecting subject matter experts with publishers.
- [HARO (Help A Reporter Out)](https://www.helpareporter.com) - The original reporter-source matching service, still the highest-volume daily press query newsletter.
- [JournoRequests](https://www.journorequests.com) - UK-focused platform aggregating journalist source requests.
- [MentionMatch](https://mentionmatch.com) - Connects B2B writers with expert sources (formerly Help a B2B Writer).
- [Qwoted](https://qwoted.com) - Source and journalist matching platform with a well-curated pool of press queries.
- [SourceBottle](https://www.sourcebottle.com) - Australian-founded platform connecting sources with journalists and bloggers globally.
- [Source of Sources](https://www.sourceofsources.com) - Peter Shankman's media query newsletter.

## Free Tools and Calculators

No-cost utilities for planning, measuring, or auditing guest post campaigns.

- [Adbassador Guest Post ROI Calculator](https://adbassador.com/guest-post-roi-calculator/) - Free calculator for estimating guest post campaign returns based on DR, traffic, and niche.
- [Ahrefs Free Backlink Checker](https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker) - Free limited view into a site's top backlinks and referring domains.
- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) - Free tool for tracking earned backlinks and referring pages.
- [Moz Link Explorer (free tier)](https://moz.com/link-explorer) - Limited free lookups of domain authority and backlinks.
- [Ubersuggest Free Tier](https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/) - Limited free backlink and keyword data.
